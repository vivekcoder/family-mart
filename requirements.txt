# Mobile Mini-Mart / Farm Management Game

## Complete Development Specification

Build an **Android-first, offline, single-player 3D mobile management game** inspired by the gameplay loop and visual philosophy of **My Mini Mart**.

The game should NOT copy proprietary assets, characters, models, textures, animations, UI, branding, or code from the original game. Use it only as inspiration for the **casual 3D management-game experience, camera style, progression, readability, and gameplay feel**.

The final result should be a polished, original casual mobile game.

---

# 1. Platform

Target platform:

**Android**

Initial version:

* Offline
* Single player
* Local save storage
* No online multiplayer
* No backend required
* No missions/quests
* No mandatory internet connection

The architecture should nevertheless be modular enough that online features could be added in a future version.

---

# 2. Core Gameplay Loop

The fundamental gameplay loop is:

```text
Farm / Produce Resources
        ↓
Process Resources
        ↓
Stock Mart Shelves
        ↓
Customers Enter
        ↓
Customers Select Products
        ↓
Customers Go To Checkout
        ↓
Player Packs / Processes Checkout
        ↓
Player Earns Money
        ↓
Player Buys Upgrades
        ↓
Farm + Production + Mart Expand
        ↓
More Products
        ↓
More Customers
        ↓
Repeat
```

The game should be easy to understand without extensive tutorials.

The physical environment itself should communicate how the production system works.

---

# 3. Starting State

The player starts with approximately:

**$10**

The exact starting amount should be configurable.

The player starts with a very small mart/farm.

Initial setup:

* One tomato shelf
* One tomato plant
* One cashier counter
* Minimal farm area
* Small mart
* Basic player character
* No advanced production machines
* No chickens
* No cows
* No wheat plots initially purchased

The starting environment should intentionally look small and underdeveloped.

The player should immediately understand:

> "I have a tiny shop that I can grow."

---

# 4. Money

Use **dollars ($)** as the game's currency.

Do not use coins.

Money is earned primarily by completing customer purchases.

Money is spent on:

* shelves
* plants
* animals
* machines
* farm plots
* fridges
* mart expansion
* farm expansion
* carrying-capacity upgrades
* other future upgrades

The money balance must persist between sessions.

# 5. Tomato Production

The starting production chain is tomatoes.

The player owns a tomato plant.

Each tomato plant:

* produces 1 tomato every second
* has a maximum storage of 3 tomatoes
* stops producing when it reaches 3
* resumes producing when tomatoes are harvested

Example:

```text
Tomato Plant
    ↓
1 tomato / second
    ↓
Maximum = 3
    ↓
Player harvests
    ↓
Plant continues producing
```

Tomatoes should be visibly present on the plant.

When harvested:

* tomato disappears from plant
* tomato is added to player's carried inventory
* small pickup animation plays

---

# 6. Tomato Shelf

The player can place tomatoes into the tomato shelf.

The shelf should visually contain actual tomatoes.

The shelf has a configurable maximum capacity.

The shelf should have visual states:

```text
Empty
Partially stocked
Full
```

If the shelf needs restocking, display a floating **!** attention indicator.

# 7. Player Carrying Capacity

The player has a limited carrying capacity.

Example:

```text
Starting capacity = configurable
```

The player can carry products harvested from farms or collected from production buildings.

Carrying capacity can be upgraded using money.

Each upgrade should:

* have a cost
* increase carrying capacity
* be represented by an upgrade pad
* visibly communicate progression

Example:

```text
Capacity Level 0 → 3 items
Capacity Level 1 → 5 items
Capacity Level 2 → 8 items
Capacity Level 3 → 12 items
...
```

The exact values must be data-driven.

# 8. Customer System

Customers periodically enter the mart.

Customers should have a shopping list generated dynamically.

### Important rule:

A customer's shopping list may contain **only products that are currently available in the mart**.

Do NOT generate a customer request for:

* an unavailable product
* a product whose shelf has not been purchased
* a product that cannot currently be sold

For example:

If only the Tomato Shelf exists:

```text
Customer:
✓ Tomato
✗ Egg
✗ Milk
✗ Bread
✗ Pizza
```

Once Egg Shelf is unlocked:

```text
Customer:
✓ Tomato
✓ Egg
```

The customer system should dynamically expand its possible shopping list as the mart expands.

---

# 9. Customer Shopping

Customer flow:

```text
Enter mart
    ↓
Generate shopping list
    ↓
Walk to required shelf
    ↓
Take required quantity
    ↓
Put products into cart
    ↓
Move to next required shelf
    ↓
After shopping → checkout
```

Customers should physically interact with shelves.

When taking an item:

```text
Shelf product
    ↓
Pickup animation
    ↓
Product appears in cart
```

Customer carts should visibly contain the products being purchased.

---

# 10. Customer Patience

Customers should have a finite waiting time at each shelf.

If a customer cannot obtain all required items within the allowed time:

```text
Customer becomes impatient
    ↓
Stops waiting at that shelf
    ↓
Moves to checkout
    ↓
Purchases whatever they have collected so far
```

The customer should NOT remain stuck indefinitely.

---

```text
    ██████████
       👤
```

```text

    😐
    ██████░░░░
       👤
```

```text

       👤
```

```text
Very low patience

    😡

       👤
```

The indicator should transition smoothly.

The customer itself should remain visually normal.

Use subtle:

* bob

as patience decreases.

Do not make the screen visually dominated by red warning states.

# 18. Unlock Dependency System

Use explicit dependency data.

A product's production source must **not become purchasable simply because that product exists in the game's configuration**.

The player must first purchase the **destination shelf/storage**.

General rule:

> **Purchasing the destination storage unlocks the source that produces the product.**

Examples:

```text
Egg Shelf
    ↓
Chicken becomes purchasable
```

```text
Milk Fridge
    ↓
Cow becomes purchasable
```

```text
Water Fridge
    ↓
Hand Pump becomes purchasable
```

```text
Cold Drinks Fridge
    ↓
Cola Machine becomes purchasable
```

```text
Bread Shelf
    ↓
Bread Oven becomes purchasable
```

```text
Pizza Shelf
    ↓
Pizza Oven becomes purchasable
```

This rule must be enforced by the unlock system and UI.

---

# 19. Unlockable Data Model

Use data-driven unlockables.

Conceptually:

```text
Unlockable
{
    id,
    displayName,
    purchasePrice,
    requiredUnlocks[],
    destinationStorage,
    prefab,
    position,
    state
}
```

For example:

```text
Chicken
{
    id: "chicken",
    requiredStorage: "egg_shelf"
}
```

The UI must not display a purchase option if the prerequisites are not met.

* lighting
* animation quality

# 60. Character Quality

Characters should have:

* proper proportions
* expressive faces
* varied clothing
* smooth animation
* consistent skeleton/rig
* multiple visual variants

The Baker should have a chef/baker hat and apron.

---

# 61. Animation Quality

Animations must be smooth and responsive.

Avoid abrupt transitions such as:

```text
Idle → instant walk
Walk → instant stop
Stop → instant pickup
```

Use short transitions.

Add subtle secondary motion:

* arm movement
* body movement
* head movement
* product movement
* machine movement

Do not make animations so long that they slow down gameplay.

---

```text
❗ Tomato ready

😠 Customer impatient
```

They must remain readable from normal gameplay distance.

# 63. No Excessive Warning Colors

```text
Entire customer → red
Entire shelf → red
Entire machine → red
```

Use:

```text
Normal object

floating state indicator

subtle animation
```

Red should primarily be used for warning/emotion icons.

* dark environments
* photorealistic lighting

* dramatic shadows

Use:

* subtle shadows

* mild highlights

```text

🐄 → produces milk

🌾 → produces wheat

🏭 → processes resources

🛒 → customers shop

🧾 → checkout

❄️ → refrigerated products
```

The player should see the world evolve as they spend money.

```text

    ↓

    ↓
More Animals
    ↓
More Farm
    ↓
More Machines
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
```

The final experience should feel like:

**"I started with almost nothing, and I physically built this entire mini-mart and farm."**

Do not attempt to build the entire game as one monolithic implementation.

Build each system as an independent, reusable component.

Important systems should be modular:

```text
PlayerController
CustomerController
CustomerAI
InventorySystem
ProductSystem
ShelfSystem
CheckoutSystem
ProductionSystem
RecipeSystem
AnimalSystem
CropSystem
UpgradeSystem
UnlockSystem
ExpansionSystem
AttentionIndicatorSystem
```
